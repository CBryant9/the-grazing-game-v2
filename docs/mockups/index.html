Render styles: same test scene, five ways

Test scene: Paddock 7 split with a tape so 300 head graze a strip of short green feed at high density. Paddock 3 and 9 rank and tall (dry tops, green at the base, seed heads). Paddock 2 and 6 leafy green. Paddock 5 oats in rows. Paddock 8 fallow. Troughs, bore and fences. Left: zoomed out (whole farm). Right: zoomed in (a few paddocks close).

Recommendation: S3 art with S5 level-of-detail behaviour

Cows never blur into grass: dark outlines and drop shadows. Big mobs collapse into one marker with a head count when zoomed out, and spread into individual cows when zoomed in. Grass reads as clean fields at a distance and as clumps up close. Height must still read (docs/VISUALS.md): rank looks tall with green only at the base, short looks short, crops sit in rows, fallow is worked brown soil. The other tabs use this style.
